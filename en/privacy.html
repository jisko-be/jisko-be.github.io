This website

This website is static. It uses no cookies, no forms, and no fonts or content loaded from a third-party service. The only external script is the audience measurement described below.

It is hosted by Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.), which also runs the DNS and the content delivery network (CDN) for the jisko.be domain. Every visit therefore goes through Cloudflare's servers, which see visitors' IP addresses. Like any host, Cloudflare may record them for security purposes and to meet its legal obligations; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Audience measurement

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits in aggregate: page views, country, browser and referring site. The service sets no cookie and uses no persistent identifier; IP addresses are not kept for measurement. Cloudflare, Inc. acts as a processor and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Jisko's legitimate interest in knowing how its site is used. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Emails

If you write to Jisko, the address and message are used only to reply and to follow up on the request. They are neither sold nor shared, and are kept for the duration of the exchange, or longer only if a legal obligation requires it (for example, accounting for an engagement).
