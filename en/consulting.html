Consulting

Jonathan Schoreels works on long assignments, usually inside the client's team. Common assignments:

  • auditing an application or a platform: architecture, code quality, maintainability, security;
  • persistent technical problems, including taking apart an old or third-party component to see how it works;
  • leading a team of developers: working practices, technical choices, liaison with other teams and management;
  • tooling around the code: continuous integration and deployment, monitoring, alerts.

He prototypes quickly to test an idea, then goes deep on performance and optimisation.

Technologies: Java and Spring Boot, Apache Camel, JMS and AMQP messaging, Docker Swarm and Kubernetes, AWS and Azure. He also uses Claude Code and Codex to move faster and check more.

Recent assignments

He works in the public and private sectors.

  • since 2024 technical architect in the public sector, on critical projects;
  • 2021–2024 team lead of eight developers at an international professional-information publisher;
  • 2019–2021 Java technical lead at a Belgian energy and telecommunications group.
