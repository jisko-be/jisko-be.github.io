About and contact

Background

Jonathan Schoreels holds a master's degree in computer science from the University of Mons (UMONS), awarded magna cum laude in 2014. During his studies he was a teaching assistant for the programming and algorithms course (Java and Python) and tutored numerical analysis and computer science. He took additional courses at the ULB in Brussels: computer security, combinatorial optimisation, graphs and networks.

In autumn 2013 he did a research internship at McGill University in Montreal, in Prof. Jörg Kienzle's software engineering lab. He studied Mammoth, a massively multiplayer online game framework built for experimenting with distributed systems and artificial intelligence. He also explored AIs that learn by watching human players.

Before founding Jisko in 2019, he worked as a consultant for VOO and Le Forem.

Method and learning

He is interested in personal organisation and skill development: continuous learning, discipline, communication and leading teams. He prefers understanding the fundamentals over piling up recipes. He keeps training, for example with Coursera's Machine Learning specialisation.

He is also learning Japanese. Spaced repetition led him to contribute to the open-source tools on this site.
